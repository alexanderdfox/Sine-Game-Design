# Photosensitive Epilepsy Warning

**WARNING: PHOTOSENSITIVITY / EPILEPSY**

A very small percentage of people may experience seizures or severe dizziness when exposed to certain flashing lights, rapid patterns, or oscillating visual effects. This software includes animated sine-wave graphics, oscillating colors, rapid motion, and oscilloscope-style displays that may trigger photosensitive seizures even in people with no prior history of epilepsy.

**If you or anyone in your family has an epileptic condition or has experienced seizures, consult a physician before use.** Stop using this software immediately and seek medical help if you experience dizziness, altered vision, eye or muscle twitching, loss of awareness, disorientation, any involuntary movement, or convulsions.

## Full Legal Notice

A minority of people may experience epileptic seizures or blackouts when exposed to certain light patterns or flashing lights, including while using video games, interactive software, or visual simulation tools. These effects may occur even in persons who have never had a previous seizure or epileptic episode.

This software (**Sine Game Design** and related modules including Studio, Vector, Matrix, and Game Studio) produces visual content that may include, without limitation:

- rapidly oscillating or pulsing geometric patterns  
- high-contrast sine-wave and vector animations  
- flashing or strobing color changes  
- oscilloscope-style and phosphor-style displays  
- continuous motion and particle effects

### Recommendations for safer use

- Use in a well-lit room.  
- Keep a reasonable distance from the screen.  
- Take regular breaks.  
- Avoid use when tired or under the influence of substances that may lower seizure threshold.  
- If symptoms occur, stop immediately and rest.
